## Round 12 — the real waist-up bug: spine twist accumulated every frame

Root cause found from the second screenshot (head and arms gone, torso and legs present). The two-handed
rifle pose is applied every frame, and it did spine2.rotation.y += yaw * 0.45. That ADDS a little
twist on every frame, so the spine wound up without bound; the head and arms are children of that bone,
so they orbited out of the torso while the hips/legs stayed put — exactly "invisible from the waist up".
Weapon swap changed the aim yaw feeding that pose, which is why Q appeared to trigger it.

Fix: assign the pose absolutely (
otation.y = yaw * 0.45), and keep the NaN guards. Regression now
asserts the spine stays finite and within ±0.6 rad after a swap. Verified: spine y −0.001 after aiming +
swap, full body visible.

only lights on the operator (key/rim) left it black against black ground; the legs caught the ground
glow, so the torso read as gone. The Q swap was a coincidence with the wave starting, not the cause.

(2.2 + darkness * 5.5). Verified on FOUNDRY at 99.9 % darkness: torso, head and weapon stay readable

phase can never NaN a bone and collapse the rig.

## Round 6 — enemy fire you can see and dodge

Design problem reported: "enemies have no shoot animation… character has no real way to avoid shots and
just dies."

**Before:** `rangedShot` / `minigun` were instant damage rolls with no visual — the only counterplay was
not being in range, and the pilot had no dodge.

**Now:** enemy fire is a real projectile.
- muzzle flash at the shooter + a recoil pose (arm bones and spine kick back, `applyTwoHandedRiflePose`
  gained a recoil term)
- a visible bolt in flight (elongated additive box, 12 m/s rifle, 19 m/s mech minigun), aimed with a
  small lead and spread
- bolts are stopped by cover (`segmentHitsCollider`), expire after their flight time, and hit a
  0.55 m operator capsule
- the pilot computes closest approach for every bolt and **sidesteps perpendicular** to it
  (`dodge-shot` mode, full throttle) — the sign bug that made every approach look like a receding
  shot is fixed and called out in the code
- HUD flash on taking a bolt hit; `getCombat()` exposes fired / hit / incoming for tests

Measured with three riflemen at 10 m (`tools/enemyshots.mjs`): **6 bolts fired, 0 hits, 82 frames of
incoming threat, 1.25 s spent dodging** — before the fix it was 6/6 hits, 0 dodges. Screenshot
`docs/evidence/enemy-fire.png` shows two riflemen firing bolts while the operator sidesteps.

Balance consequence: with chip damage dodgeable, 10 seeded runs moved to median **173 s**, **5 cards**,
boss 100 %, **cleared 40 %** (was 105 s / 4 / 30 %). Slightly over the survival and clear targets; the
next lever is enemy bolt damage or fire cadence, not HP.

## Round 5 — Q-swap damage bug, clear definition, radar/feed/hit marker

**Bug report from Justin: "switch weapons with Q -> no damage output." Reproduced and fixed.**
Secondary is pierce (pierceCount 3). The pierce branch measured each candidate's projection from the
**target**, then required `proj > 0` — so the target's own projection was 0 and it was excluded. A
pierce weapon only ever damaged enemies lined up behind the thing it was aiming at; with no such
enemy, the shot fired, consumed ammo and dealt 0. Measured before: 4 s after Q -> 0 damage, 0 kills.
After: 204 damage, 3 kills in 4 s. The target is now always first in the line, plus up to
`pierceCount` behind it. Regress now asserts a Q-swap deals damage, so it cannot come back.

**Clear definition:** "arena cleared" now means the boss is dead (it used to mean surviving the ~12
minute overtime line, impossible in a 100 s run). Results title flips to `SECTOR CLEARED`, the run
banks as cleared, and `TUNE-02` measured clear **30 %** (target 20-35). Two causes of unkillable
bosses were fixed on the way: a live boss was not prioritised by the auto-gun at any distance, and
ground bosses were scaled by `bossHp()` (34-damage-rifle curve) into thousands of HP —
`TUNING.boss.hpScale` (0.2) now sizes the spawned ground boss.

**HUD:** radar (26 m, up = facing, boss pips amber), kill feed (last three), screen-centre hit marker
(red on kill), low-integrity wash via the flash layer, compact phone layout.

**Gate hardening:** `node --check file.js` parses bare `.js` as CommonJS and missed a broken template
literal that made the whole page fail to load. `tools/verify_syntax.py` now copies every source to
`.mjs` and checks it as a module too.

## Round 3 — tactical movement, enemy attacks, aerial boss

**Movement was redesigned at Justin's request** ("always running in a glitchy pattern"). The pilot
now fights in beats instead of strafing non-stop:

- `HOLD` — plant, face the target, let the gun work (throttle 0)
- `REPOSITION` — a 0.62 s dash to a new angle, then plant again
- `ADVANCE` / `BACKOFF` — bounding moves: 0.9 s push, 1.9-2.5 s pause to shoot
- `RETREAT` — 0.8 s bounding retreat when integrity is low
- `DODGE` — boss telegraph break-off

The renderer now eases velocity (`k = 10`) instead of snapping position, so starts and stops are
smooth.

Measured (`node tools/movement.mjs`): **82.8 m travelled per 60 s** (target 55-90), **0 frozen
frames**, camera push **0 m**, 18-39 hold beats per run, max frame velocity change 1.4-1.6 m/s
(eased, no snaps).

**Enemy attacks were stubs.** Only melee ever reached the player — that is why an autonomous pilot
could never lose. `rangedShot`, `dive`, `shell`, `stomp`, `minigun`, `charge` and `paint` now apply
damage/effects with line-of-sight and range gates.

**TUNE-04 gunship:** aerial boss now uses `TUNING.boss.gunship.hp` (900) instead of the ground
`bossHp()` (+2600 flat). Measured TTK **11 s** with flak (target 8-15 s), firing from **6.75 m**
altitude. The single-shot path also had to use `effectiveDamage` — anti-air/lit/explosive modifiers
were being computed and then ignored.

**TUNE-02 difficulty:** `difficulty.hpMult = 3.0`, `hpScaling = 0.24`; runs now end in death instead

## Bugs found by measuring

1. **The game clock ran at 2x.** `main.js` incremented `beatDirector.runTime` and
   `beatDirector.update(dt)` incremented it again. Every beat, spawn rate, difficulty ramp, XP/s and
   boss threshold was double speed. Removed; a 60 s wall-clock run now reports 60 s of game time.
2. **Results screen printed raw floats** (`60.5000000000000049`). `director.js` now rounds to one
   decimal.
3. **Objective banner overlapped the darkness banner.** Darkness now uses `HUD_SLOTS.statusBanner`.
4. **Air-damage read `effectiveDamage` before it was declared** (TDZ throw on any flak shot). Fixed.
5. **Death never reached the results screen.** `hurt()` parked the run in `dead`; fixed.
