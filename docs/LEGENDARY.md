**Introduction:**

The Legendary Loadout items in *Forge Smoke* are exclusive, high-tier upgrades that transform the core gameplay mechanics of the cyborg-fighter management system. Each item fundamentally alters either movement or defence, ensuring that players must balance risk and reward in their tactical decisions. These items are earned through specific arena clearances, encouraging strategic progression and deep engagement with the game's core systems. Unlike standard upgrades, Legendary items introduce meaningful tradeoffs, forcing players to rethink their playstyle based on mission demands and battlefield conditions.
